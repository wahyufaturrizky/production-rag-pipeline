# Personal Loans

## KTA personal loan
NusaBank offers unsecured personal loans (KTA) from IDR 5,000,000 up to IDR 200,000,000 with tenors of 12 to 60 months. The interest rate starts from 0.99% flat per month depending on your credit profile.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be Indonesian citizens aged 21 to 55 with a monthly income of at least IDR 5,000,000 and an active NusaBank account for at least 3 months.

## Early repayment
Loans can be repaid early with a penalty of 3% of the outstanding principal.

## Products not offered
NusaBank does not currently offer mortgages (KPR), vehicle loans or credit cards.
