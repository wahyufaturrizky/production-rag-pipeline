# Savings and Deposits

## Tabungan Plus savings account
Tabungan Plus pays 3.25% p.a. on balances above IDR 10,000,000 and 1.5% p.a. on balances below that. Interest is calculated daily and credited monthly. There is no monthly administration fee.

## Time deposits
Time deposits are available for 1, 3, 6 and 12 month tenors with a minimum placement of IDR 5,000,000. The 12-month rate is 4.75% p.a. Early withdrawal forfeits the interest for the current period.

## Deposit insurance
Eligible deposits are guaranteed by LPS (Indonesia Deposit Insurance Corporation) up to IDR 2,000,000,000 per customer, provided the interest rate does not exceed the LPS guarantee rate.
