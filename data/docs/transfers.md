# Transfers

## Daily transfer limits
Daily transfer limits depend on your account tier: Silver IDR 25,000,000, Gold IDR 100,000,000 and Priority IDR 500,000,000. Limits reset at 00:00 WIB.

## BI-FAST interbank transfers
BI-FAST transfers to other Indonesian banks cost IDR 2,500 per transaction and arrive in real time, 24/7. The maximum amount per BI-FAST transaction is IDR 250,000,000.

## RTGS transfers
For amounts above IDR 250,000,000, use RTGS. RTGS transfers cost IDR 25,000 and are processed on working days between 08:00 and 15:00 WIB.

## International transfers
International SWIFT transfers cost IDR 150,000 per transaction plus correspondent bank fees, which are charged by intermediary banks and vary by route. Funds usually arrive in 1 to 3 working days. Transfers to Singapore, Malaysia and Australia support same-day delivery when sent before 11:00 WIB.
