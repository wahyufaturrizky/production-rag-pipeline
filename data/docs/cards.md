# NusaBank Debit Cards

NusaBank is a fictional Indonesian digital bank used for demonstration purposes.

## Lost or stolen cards
If your debit card is lost or stolen, block it immediately in the NusaBank app under Cards > Block card, or call the 24/7 hotline 1500-321. Blocking takes effect instantly and cannot be undone; a new card number is issued.

## Card replacement
A replacement card is delivered within 5 working days to your registered address. The replacement fee is IDR 15,000, waived for Gold and Priority customers.

## Card limits
The default daily ATM withdrawal limit is IDR 10,000,000 and the daily debit purchase limit is IDR 50,000,000. Limits can be lowered in the app at any time; raising them requires biometric verification.

## Online and overseas use
Online and overseas transactions are disabled by default for new cards. Enable them in the app under Cards > Security settings. Overseas transactions incur a 2% currency conversion fee.
