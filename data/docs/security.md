# Account Security

## PIN, OTP and passwords
NusaBank staff will never ask for your PIN, OTP or password by phone, chat, email or social media. Never share them with anyone.

## Resetting your PIN
Reset your ATM PIN in the app under Cards > Reset PIN and verify with face biometrics. A PIN reset at a branch requires your KTP (national ID card).

## Suspicious activity
If you notice a transaction you did not make, block your card immediately and report it through the app or the 24/7 hotline 1500-321. Report phishing messages to phishing@nusabank.example.

## Device binding
Your NusaBank account can be active on one phone at a time. Logging in on a new device requires face biometrics and deactivates the previous device.
