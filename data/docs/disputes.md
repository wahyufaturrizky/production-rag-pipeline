# Disputes and Complaints

## Disputing a transaction
To dispute a duplicate, incorrect or unauthorised transaction, open the app, go to Transactions, select the transaction and tap Dispute. Disputes must be raised within 45 days of the transaction date.

## Dispute timelines
Disputes are investigated within 14 working days. For card transactions, the disputed amount may be provisionally credited to your account after 5 working days while the investigation continues.

## Formal complaints
Formal complaints receive a ticket number and a response from a human agent within 2 working days. If a complaint is not resolved within 20 working days, customers may escalate it to OJK (Indonesia Financial Services Authority).
